Our Mission

We believe the browser new tab screen is prime real estate. Instead of wasting it on a blank page or a search bar, we wanted to create a space that feels like home.

Why Nestpane?

Nestpane was born out of frustration with cluttered, ad-heavy new tab extensions. We wanted a dashboard that is lightning fast, beautiful to look at, and strictly private. Your data never leaves your browser.

The Design

Our branding revolves around the concept of a "Nest" (a cozy, organized home base) and a "Pane" (a window or glass panel). The logo is a nest, cradling two eggs — modular, customizable, and structured. We use a deep charcoal background to reduce eye strain, with vibrant orange accents to keep the energy high.

Open Source & Free

Nestpane is free and its source code is open on GitHub under the MIT License — read it, fork it, or send a pull request. We're committed to keeping the core experience free forever, and we rely on community support and feedback to push updates that matter to you.
