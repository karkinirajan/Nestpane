Release History

Changelog

What has shipped in Nestpane, release by release.

2.1.1

Latest October 2026
  • First-login Cloud Backup Override: Signing into Google on a new browser or fresh install now reliably pulls your cloud backup from Google Drive appDataFolder and overrides the initial default state instead of letting fresh-install timestamps overwrite your cloud backup.
  • Instant Progressive Drive Sync: Local edits now push to Google Drive within 500ms with an in-flight queue and automatic tab-hide flush, ensuring rapid edits and popup bookmark saves are never dropped.
  • Human-Crafted Obsidian & Paper Design System: Refined typography (Inter, Instrument Serif, JetBrains Mono), 6 complete surface themes (Obsidian, Paper, Nord, Catppuccin Mocha, Gruvbox, Solarized), 1px hairline borders, and unified extension popup styling.

2.1.0

October 21–22, 2026
  • Autosave on tab hide: Notes and journal entries autosave as you type and flush immediately when the tab goes to the background or closes.
  • Brave & Chromium OAuth session persistence: Google sign-in sessions persist across new tabs on Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, and Arc.
  • Versioned storage migrations: Automatic pre-migration snapshot backup and rollback protection in chrome.storage.local.
  • Hosted Web Dashboard: Try Nestpane directly in the browser at /dashboard/.

2.0.1 and earlier
