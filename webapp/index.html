Purpose of Nestpane

Why Nestpane exists, and exactly what it uses your Google account for if you choose to sign in.

1. Reclaim the new tab

Every new browser tab is usually wasted screen space. Nestpane turns that blank page into one local workspace for the tools you check dozens of times a day — bookmarks, running notes, Nestodo tasks, a focus timer, and daily habits — without requiring an account or a backend server.

2. Narrowly-scoped Google Drive backup

Google sign-in is entirely optional and unlocks one specific feature: automatic backup and multi-device sync of your Nestpane state inside a private, hidden appDataFolder in your own Google Drive (drive.appdata). Nestpane cannot see, list, edit, or delete any of your regular Google Drive files.

3. Offline by default

Everything — bookmarks, notes, tasks, timer sessions, journal entries, and habits — works 100% offline in your browser's local storage. When you sign in on a new browser, Nestpane automatically restores your cloud backup over the default setup. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Capabilities

Built for daily velocity, not visual noise.

Every tool on your new tab is designed to respond instantaneously from local storage and stay out of your way until needed.

Workspaces & Quick Access

Separate bookmarks into dedicated workspaces (Personal, Work, Research) and pin high-frequency sites with customizable icon/label density.

Markdown Notes & Nestodo

Write tagged Markdown notes with instant autosave on tab hide, and manage tasks on a 3-column Kanban board with scheduled reminders.

Focus Timer & Site Blocker

Run Pomodoro focus sessions with on-device declarativeNetRequest distraction blocking and track daily habit streaks.

Instant Google Drive Sync (optional)

Progressive local changes sync to your hidden Drive appDataFolder in milliseconds, and fresh browser installs automatically restore your cloud backup on first sign-in.

Universal Command Palette (⌘K)

Jump to any open tab, Chrome bookmark, note, task, or browser history entry from the keyboard — with optional BYOK Claude AI actions.

Daily Journal & Tab Sessions

Keep a date-indexed personal journal with mood tracking, and snapshot entire windows of open tabs to restore later in one click.
