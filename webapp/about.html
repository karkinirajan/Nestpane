Philosophy

Built like a physical desk, not an ad billboard.

The browser new tab screen is opened dozens of times a day. Instead of wasting it on a blank page or a bloated feed, Nestpane turns it into a calm, local-first workspace that respects your attention and your data.

Why Nestpane?

Nestpane was born out of frustration with slow, account-gated, or ad-heavy new tab extensions. We wanted a workspace that loads in zero milliseconds from local storage, feels calm and intentional to use all day, and keeps your data strictly in your own hands.

Craft & Visual Restraint

The name combines a Nest (a personal, organized home base) and a Pane (a crisp architectural window). Our default Obsidian Dark and Warm Paper themes use warm mineral neutrals, 1px hairline dividers, tabular monospace numerals, and a restrained Terracotta Ember accent to prevent eye strain across long sessions.

Open Source & Local-First

Nestpane is free and its source code is open on GitHub under the MIT License. You can inspect every permission, audit the Google Drive appDataFolder sync engine and AES-GCM encryption, or build it from source yourself.
