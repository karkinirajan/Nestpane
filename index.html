What is Nestpane?

Nestpane is a free Google Chrome browser extension. Once installed, it replaces the default blank/search "new tab" page with a personal productivity dashboard: your bookmarks organized into workspaces, a notes editor, a to-do list, a calendar, a Kanban board, a journal, a habit tracker, and a focus timer — all in a single page you already see dozens of times a day.

All of this data is stored locally in your own browser by default — Nestpane has no servers of its own and does not require creating an account. Two features are entirely optional and off unless you turn them on: signing in with your Google account to back up your data to a private folder in your own Google Drive, and adding your own Anthropic API key to enable AI-assisted features like a command bar and daily briefing. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
